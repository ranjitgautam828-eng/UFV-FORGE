The decision

Back the opportunity. Keep the power to say no.

UFV FORGE is a proposed public investment in practical computing skills and regional collaboration. The recommendation is conditional: fund the plan only when demand, affordability and operating support are evidenced.

Condition 1

Prove demand

Run the student platform pilot and secure at least 10 committed industry partners before the June 2028 design gate.

Condition 2

Protect affordability

Keep the cost estimate within 5% of baseline at the first gate, use monthly cost reviews and require steering approval for contingency use.

Condition 3

Secure the bridge

A written UFV commitment of up to $7M for operating ramp-up shortfalls must be signed before the first gate. The $5M project contingency cannot cover operating deficits.

The questions that matter

What should decision-makers test?

Is this a commercial investment?

No. The report frames it as a public-value investment. A projected $0.5M annual surplus would imply roughly 200 years to recover $100M through surplus alone; the case rests on skills, research, partnerships and operating sustainability.

Can revenue cover the running costs?

The full-operation target is $6M annual revenue against approximately $5.5M annual operating cost. That is a forecast, not a guarantee. The base case targets full coverage by 2032; the conservative case by 2034.

What happens if a gate is missed?

The project can be redirected, held for further testing or stopped. Construction and opening are not automatic simply because the calendar says they should happen.

Where is the detailed evidence?

The full project charter and management pre-plan contains the assumptions, detailed budget, schedule, technical requirements and risk register.
