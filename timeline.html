Proposed schedule · 2026–2030

From a pilot to a working hub.

The target is to open in August 2030, before the September 1, 2030 deadline. A two-month buffer is reserved for critical-path delays.

Three formal kill points—not four.

Each is a documented go/no-go decision. Other project reviews and monthly controls support these gates but are not formal kill points in the project charter.

Nov 2026 – Mar 2027

Initiation and charter

Confirm governance, scope, responsibilities, initial risks and the project charter.

Jan – Jun 2027

Planning and requirements

Gather requirements from students, faculty, UFV IT, businesses and regulators; define acceptance criteria and the initial business case.

May – Dec 2027

Student platform pilot

Build and test the practice platform in existing space while recruiting industry partners and the core team.

Jul 2027 – Jun 2028

Building and technical design

Complete design, permits, technical requirements and consultation before major construction commitments.

June 2028 Kill point 1

Design and affordability review

Go criteria include approved design, permits issued or on track, cost estimate within 5% of baseline, at least 10 committed partners, and a signed UFV bridge commitment of up to $7M.

Jul – Oct 2028

Contracts and supplier selection

Competitive procurement, contract awards and supplier planning.

Nov 2028 – Dec 2029

Construction

Build the facility, laboratories, studios and server room. Monthly cost and schedule reviews continue.

Jan – Mar 2030

Equipment installation

Install networking, student servers, cyber range, GPU/HPC equipment and IoT systems.

May 2030 Kill point 2

Systems and security review

All major systems must pass tests; independent security testing must pass; no critical defects can remain open.

Jun – Jul 2030

Project buffer

Two months reserved to absorb critical-path delays before final close-out.

August 2030 Kill point 3

Final acceptance and opening

Confirm deliverables, handover documents and first-year operating budget before the hub is accepted and opens to students.
