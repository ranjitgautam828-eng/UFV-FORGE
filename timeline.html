The Timeline

From approval to opening.

A realistic path to September 1, 2030 — with four strict kill-point gates.

Nov 2026

Initiation & charter

Project manager assigned. Industry board recruitment begins. Pilot platform development starts in existing space.

Mar 2027

Gate 1: Charter approval Kill-point

Governance structure and initial partner commitments verified.

Q1–Q4 2027

Pilot platform & hiring

Pilot platform tested in existing space. Core staff hired. Facility design begins.

Dec 2027

Gate 2: Pilot results & partners Kill-point

Pilot platform results and partner commitments reviewed. If partners or costs fail criteria, scope is reduced or project is halted.

Q1–Q4 2028

Design, permits & procurement

Facility design and permits finalized. Labs & server procurement begins.

Apr 2028

Gate 3: Design sign-off Kill-point

Final design approval. Construction proceeds only if all permits and consultation are complete.

Q1 2029–Q2 2030

Construction & installation

Facility construction. Labs, server room, and network installation. Connection Fund launches.

Jun 2029

Gate 4: Mid-construction cost review Kill-point

Halt or rescope if overrun exceeds 10%.

Q3 2030

Commissioning & move-in

Revenue services launch. Hub operational.

Aug 2030

Hub opens to students

Facility fully operational. Labs, platform, and industry wing live.

Sep 1, 2030

Closeout & benefits review

Project closed. Final benefits report submitted.
