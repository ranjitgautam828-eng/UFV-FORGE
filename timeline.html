Proposed schedule · 2026–2030

Prove demand before committing to construction.

The sequence starts with a small platform pilot, moves through design and construction, then tests every major system before opening.

Target opening: August 2030.

The fictional project deadline is September 1, 2030. A two-month buffer is reserved ahead of final acceptance.

Nov 2026 – Jun 2027

Define the need

Confirm scope, gather requirements from students, faculty, UFV IT and industry, and establish the initial operating model.

May – Dec 2027

Test the idea early

Launch a student platform pilot in existing space while recruiting partners and forming the core team.

Jul 2027 – Jun 2028

Design the hub

Complete technical and building design, permits, consultation and cost planning before construction is authorized.

June 2028 · Formal gate 1

Go / redirect / stop

Proceed only with an approved design, costs within 5% of baseline, permits issued or on track, at least 10 committed partners and a signed UFV bridge commitment.

Decision gate
Jul 2028 – Dec 2029

Procure and build

Run competitive procurement, sign contracts and construct the facility. Monthly cost and schedule reviews continue throughout.

Jan – May 2030 · Formal gate 2

Install, test, secure

Install servers and labs, test performance, complete independent security testing and close all critical defects before proceeding.

Decision gate
Jun – Jul 2030

Protect the finish line

Use the two-month schedule buffer to absorb critical-path delays—not as extra time for unplanned scope.

August 2030 · Formal gate 3

Accept and open

Confirm deliverables, handover documents and an approved first-year operating budget before formal acceptance.
