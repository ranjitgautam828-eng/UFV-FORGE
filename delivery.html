Project Controls

Ambition needs accountability.

A proposed delivery system that connects scope, schedule, cost, risk, and benefits — with clear owners and decision gates before major commitments.

01 / Governance

One accountable owner per decision.

The proposed governance model separates oversight, delivery, and specialist advice so risks are surfaced early and decisions are documented.

A · Sponsor / Steering Committee

Accountable for outcomes

Owns the business case, approves baseline changes, resolves escalated issues, and authorizes stage-gate decisions.

B · Project Manager / PMO

Accountable for delivery

Integrates the schedule, cost forecast, dependencies, risk register, decisions log, and monthly status reporting.

C · Workstream Leads

Responsible for outputs

Lead facilities, IT/HPC, cybersecurity, finance, procurement, operations, and stakeholder engagement deliverables.

D · Advisory & Community

Consulted on decisions

Provides input on industry relevance, accessibility, Indigenous engagement, privacy, sustainability, and community impacts.

02 / Stage Gates

Earn the right to proceed.

Each formal kill point requires evidence, a named decision owner, and a recorded go / revise / stop decision. Intermediate reviews support these three formal decisions.

G1 · Mar 2027

Charter and feasibility

Approve the scope statement, governance, initial risk exposure, high-level schedule, and partner discovery plan.

Evidence required: Signed charter, assumptions log, initial business case.
G2 · Dec 2027

Demand and operating model Pilot

Confirm the pilot demonstrates user demand, operational feasibility, and credible partner commitments.

Evidence required: Pilot results, partner letters, revised operating forecast.
Kill point 1 · Jun 2028

Design, permits and procurement Gate

Do not authorize construction until design maturity, permitting, consultation, procurement strategy, and cost baseline are approved.

Evidence required: Approved design, permit status, tender strategy, cost plan.
Monthly monitoring · 2029

Cost and schedule recovery Delivery

Reforecast remaining work, test contingency adequacy, and approve recovery or scope options when thresholds are breached.

Evidence required: Forecast at completion, change log, recovery options.
03 / Risk Management

Manage uncertainty before it becomes cost.

Initial qualitative risks for planning. Ratings are provisional and must be validated with project stakeholders.

High Risk · Finance + Facilities

Construction cost escalation

Scope or market pricing exceeds the approved baseline.

Response: Independent estimate, contingency rules, staged procurement, and value engineering.

High Risk · Partnerships

Insufficient partner demand

Revenue or placement assumptions are not supported.

Response: Validate demand through pilot, letters of intent, and conservative revenue scenarios.

Medium Risk · IT / HPC

Technology obsolescence

Compute and security equipment ages before opening.

Response: Modular architecture, staged purchasing, lifecycle refresh plan, and vendor-neutral requirements.

High Risk · Engagement

Permitting or consultation delay

Approvals or meaningful engagement take longer than planned.

Response: Start early, track dependencies, resource engagement, and keep schedule float.

Medium Risk · Security

Cybersecurity / privacy incident

Labs or student-operated systems expose sensitive data.

Response: Threat modelling, network segmentation, least privilege, incident exercises, and privacy review.

04 / Benefits Realization

Measure outcomes, not just completion.

Targets below are proposed measures to baseline and approve during planning — not achieved results or guaranteed forecasts.

Student Experience · Academic Lead

Work-integrated learning

Track student project participation, paid placements, certifications, and completion of applied lab projects.

Industry Value · Partnerships Lead

Partner engagement

Track active partners, repeat projects, partner satisfaction, and conversion from pilot to ongoing collaboration.

Financial · Finance Lead

Operating performance

Report earned revenue, operating cost, utilization, funding gap, and forecast-to-actual variance quarterly.

Safe Operations · Operations + Security

Service & security

Track service availability, incident response exercises, remediation age, access reviews, and privacy findings.

The Operating Principle

Make the evidence visible.
Make the decision explicit.

Before the proposal advances, validate assumptions, assign named owners, approve measurable thresholds, and baseline the integrated plan.
