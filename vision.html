The Vision

A hub built for the future of work.

UFV FORGE combines hands-on education, applied research, startup incubation, and industry partnership under one sustainable roof.

The Connection Engine

Businesses post real problems. Students build real solutions.

Businesses post real problems; professors supervise; students build solutions for pay or credit. A dedicated Connection Fund pays student fellowships, faculty chairs, and matches industry money.

This isn't a simulation. Students operate and secure live servers, maintain real websites, and work directly with the local tech economy before they graduate.

The Self-Sustaining Model

Built to outlive the grant.

The hub earns income from hosting, memberships, sponsored projects, training, and grants, backed by a long-term endowment, so it keeps running and growing after the federal money is spent.
