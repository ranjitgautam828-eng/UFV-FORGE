The idea

Turn learning into proof of skill.

UFV FORGE is designed around one simple shift: students should graduate having built, operated and secured real technology—not only studied it.

Explore the experience →
From classroom to capability

Learn it. Build it. Run it.

Students move from guided practice to operating their own sandbox environments, deploying websites and services, and learning how to protect what they build.

The goal is not to replace coursework. It is to give coursework a place to become practical experience.

A regional connection

Make local talent easier to find.

Local organizations can bring defined technical challenges into a supervised environment. Students gain experience, faculty guide the work, and partners get a clearer way to collaborate with emerging talent.

FORGE is a proposed bridge between education and the technology needs of the Fraser Valley.

The guiding principle

Infrastructure is the tool. Capability is the outcome.

The measure of success is not how impressive the building looks. It is what students can do, what research becomes possible, and whether the hub can keep operating over time.
