After opening

The operating model has to stand on its own.

The plan estimates $5.5M in annual operating costs and targets $6M in annual revenue at full operation.

Cost base

$5.5M / year

Staffing, power and hardware, security, and student operations.

Revenue target

$6.0M / year

Memberships, sponsored projects, research, hosting, endowment income and training.

Ramp-up protection

Up to $7M

A written UFV bridge commitment is a condition before the first formal construction gate.

Be clear about the risk.

These are planning estimates, not guaranteed income. The conservative scenario targets full operating-cost coverage by 2034, with a forecast cumulative ramp-up shortfall of $6.6M.
