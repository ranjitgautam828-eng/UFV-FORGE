Inside the Hub

Three technical pillars. One connected hub.

AI & HPC, cybersecurity, makerspace, and industry — under one roof, for one purpose: turning students into the workforce the Fraser Valley already needs.

01 · Advanced Labs

Cyber Range, GPU/HPC & IoT

A hands-on cyber range for security practice, a GPU/HPC cluster for AI and data work, and an IoT/embedded lab. Every lab runs real equipment, not simulations only.

$20M

02 · Platform

Student-Run Servers

Every student gets a personal sandbox server. Students operate, secure, and maintain live websites and services (inspired by pwn.college).

$5M

03 · Connection

Connection Engine

Businesses post real problems; professors supervise; students build solutions for pay or credit.

$22M

04 · Sustainability

Self-Sustaining Model

The hub earns income from hosting, memberships, sponsored projects, training, and grants — backed by a $20M endowment.

$20M Endowment
